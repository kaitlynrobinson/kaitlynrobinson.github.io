Book Project

The Foreign Foundations of Civil War: International Politics and the Organization of Rebellion

Why do states create new armed groups rather than support existing ones? Drawing on a large-N dataset and archival case studies, this book introduces a theory of founding support — explaining why, how, and under what conditions states pursue this delegation strategy, and showing that foreign-founded groups prove more capable and more violent than independently formed ones.
